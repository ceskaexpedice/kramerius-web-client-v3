The “Orphan works” licence covers documents that are still protected by copyright, but whose author is unknown or cannot be traced, so it would not be possible to obtain the mandatory consent for publication. Libraries may digitise such works and make them available to the public on the basis of the European Directive 2012/28/EU on certain permitted uses of orphan works and the Czech Copyright Act No. 228/2014 Coll. (https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2000/121)

In the digital library, documents with the “Orphan works” licence behave the same as documents with the “Public domain” licence, so they can be printed and downloaded.
