All digital library users have remote access to public domain works.

The digital library contains the full texts of documents (books, journals, newspapers, etc.) that are, from the perspective of copyright law, so-called free works (no longer protected by copyright). This means that more than 70 years have passed since the death of the author and all other creators (illustrators, translators, etc.) and more than 50 years since publication, or, in the case of newspapers and journals, more than 110 years since publication. Such works are marked here as “public”. You can freely view, download, and print them.

If you have any questions, or if you feel that a document should not be publicly accessible, please write to us at digitalniknihovna@mzk.cz.
