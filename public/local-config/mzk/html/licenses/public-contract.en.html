All digital library users have remote access to contractually published works.

The MZK digital library contains some documents (books, journals, newspapers, etc.) that are still protected by copyright law, but the Moravian Library has concluded an agreement on publication with the publisher or copyright holder (or these are directly works of its employees). On the basis of this agreement, users of our digital library may freely view, download, and print such works even without logging in. It is possible that you will find these documents locked in other digital libraries.
